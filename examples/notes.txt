The connection pool was resized during the incident review on Tuesday afternoon. Engineers found that the previous limit of sixteen connections was far too low for the observed peak traffic. When every connection was busy the gateway queued new requests and tail latency climbed well past the alerting threshold within a couple of minutes. The on-call responder opened an incident bridge and pulled in the platform team to look at the saturation. The team raised the limit to thirty two and added a hard timeout so a slow upstream can no longer hold a connection open forever. A follow up load test confirmed that the new limit absorbs the measured peak with a comfortable safety margin to spare. Metrics are now exported every ten seconds so the same saturation pattern would be caught much earlier next time. The change shipped behind a feature flag and was rolled out to a single region first before the full fleet. No customer requests were dropped during the window and the error rate returned to its baseline within ten minutes. The final report was circulated to stakeholders the following morning with a complete timeline and a short list of action items.
